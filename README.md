# Lantern Warden

*Survive the endless night in the Lantern Forest.*

An action survival game that runs right in the browser, with nothing to install. You are the lantern keeper in an endless night: fight monsters, collect XP and pick a game mode.

**Play it here:** https://lantern-warden.raztresenliam.workers.dev

## How to play

- Move and aim with the crosshair
- Every kill gives XP for upgrades
- Choose your weapon before the run: Crossbow, Longbow or Lantern Staff (no switching mid-game!)
- Survive as long as you can

## Game modes

- **Survival**: monsters swarm from every side, and the field changes every 5 minutes
- **Tower Defence**: build towers and barricades with gold and protect the lantern flame
- **Boss Rush**: boss after boss, back to back
- **Just Shooting**: no towers, no lantern, pure aim
- **Hardcore**: one heart, one mistake
- **Daily Challenge**: new mutators every day
- **Custom**: mix your own mutators

## Run it locally

Download `index.html` and open it in Chrome. That's it.
